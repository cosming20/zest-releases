Zest 0.1.0 - bundled third-party components
===========================================

Zest itself is released under the MIT license (LICENSE). It runs the programs below as
separate processes; each keeps its own license, and the full texts ship inside the app
(macOS: Zest.app/Contents/Resources/Licenses, Windows: the Licenses folder of the install).
All of them are unmodified upstream builds.

Component                         macOS build              Windows build            License
FFmpeg                            8.0.1 (Homebrew)         9.0.2 (Gyan.dev full)    GPL-3.0-or-later
Pandoc                            3.12.1                   3.12.1                   GPL-2.0-or-later
Ghostscript                       10.08.0 (Homebrew)       10.08.0                  AGPL-3.0-or-later
LibreOffice                       26.2.4.2                 26.2.4.2                 MPL-2.0 and others
ONLYOFFICE x2t converter          10.0.0                   10.0.0                   AGPL-3.0
Carlito, Caladea, Liberation      -                        -                        SIL OFL 1.1
Microsoft Visual C++ runtime      -                        bundled                  VS redistribution terms

The FFmpeg builds enable GPL components (libx264, libx265 and others); the license of every
bundled library is listed next to the FFmpeg license inside the app.
